## Verified but not cited

- **Benkard's NBER version (w7127)** and **Blanchard–Summers w1950**: superseded by the published versions cited above.
- **NEA (2000), Reduction of Capital Costs of Nuclear Power Plants**: the primary source behind NEA 7530's pair-effect figure; cited through 7530, which is where the figure was verified.

## Considered and dropped

- **Andersson and Finnegan (working paper), French synthetic control**: unpublished; its 62%/20% estimates are not needed by any claim the paper makes.
- **World Nuclear Industry Status Report chronology**: the same dates are in IAEA RDS-2, the primary registry; cite the registry.
- **Kharecha and Hansen (2013), prevented mortality**: global counterfactual with contested assumptions; the German record in Jarvis et al. carries the health claim with cleaner identification.
- **European Council gas-price series**: the paper's 2022 exposure claim needs only the IEA import shares; wholesale price paths would add decimals without adding argument.
